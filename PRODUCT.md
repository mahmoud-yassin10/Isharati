# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Egyptian teachers of deaf and hard-of-hearing students, and the students themselves. Teachers work at a desk or in class, often with weak sign-language support. Students learn visually: they need to see a concept move, then check they understood.

Primary task on a teacher screen: preview a lesson, publish it, see who finished the lab challenge.
Primary task on a student screen: complete the current lesson step, especially the interactive physics lab.

## Product Purpose

Raqeeb turns Egypt’s existing school curriculum into an accessible Egyptian Sign Language lesson with a real interactive simulation. Success is a student who can change force and mass and see why acceleration changes, not a student who watched a translated paragraph.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, school-lab. Three words: measured, visual, Arabic-first.

Voice: short instructional Arabic. Name the control. Name the result. No slogans.

## Anti-references

- Dark SaaS dashboards with neon green KPIs
- Cream-and-terracotta “AI education” landing pages
- Video-hero marketing overlays
- Identical icon+heading card grids
- Chalkboard textures, apple-on-desk stock photos, emoji as icons

## Design Principles

- The lab is the interface. If the simulation is not on screen, the product is hiding.
- Show the relationship, not a metaphor for the relationship.
- One primary action per screen.
- Arabic is the language; Latin letters are for formulas (F, m, a) only.
- Deaf-first: captions, visible focus, no information by color alone, reduced-motion still leaves the lab usable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body contrast ≥4.5:1, prefer 7:1. Touch targets ≥44px. `prefers-reduced-motion` pauses the box motion but keeps sliders and the live `a` readout. Color is never the only signal (label the box “كتلة”, the arrow “قوة”). Keyboard can move sliders.
